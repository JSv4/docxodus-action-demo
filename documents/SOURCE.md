# Document provenance

The original fixture is the NVCA Model Amended and Restated Certificate of
Incorporation, dated October 1, 2025, downloaded from:

[NVCA-Model-COI-10-1-2025.docx](https://nvca.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/NVCA-Model-COI-10-1-2025.docx)

The NVCA [model legal documents page](https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/)
provides the document's context and accompanying guidance.

`original.docx` is the unmodified download. Its SHA-256 is:

```text
d75600769c12724990de48149d7a2bb161f3522daa54b1783672f93697d87d29
```

It contains 44 ZIP package parts, 234 top-level body paragraphs, 95 footnotes
with positive IDs (plus two separator entries), Word fields, bookmarks,
numbered provisions, section properties, and multiple header/footer parts.
The original contains no tables; the revised fixture adds one.

`revised.docx` is a synthetic software-testing derivative generated by
`scripts/build_fixture.py`. Aurora Robotics, Inc. and the proposed terms are
fictional. The derivative intentionally retains other model alternatives and
unfilled placeholders; it is not a completed charter or legal advice.
`changes.json` records every intentional edit and both file hashes.

Only the payloads of `word/document.xml` and `word/footnotes.xml` are changed.
All other package-part payloads, including styles, numbering, relationships,
headers and footers, remain identical to the original. ZIP serialization can
differ from the source package.
